# CU-4 Controller Known Issues

Open and resolved defects affecting released CU-4 firmware. See the release notes for feature changes.

## Issue CU4-KI-101 (affects firmware 4.1)

Symptom: Modbus registers above 40000 return stale values. Workaround: restart the controller during the next maintenance window. Resolved in firmware 4.2.

## Issue CU4-KI-102 (affects firmware 4.2)

Symptom: display freezes after 49 days of uptime. Workaround: restart the controller during the next maintenance window. Resolved in firmware 4.3.

## Issue CU4-KI-103 (affects firmware 4.3)

Symptom: BACnet device ID resets after power loss. Workaround: restart the controller during the next maintenance window. Resolved in firmware 4.4.

## Issue CU4-KI-104 (affects firmware 4.4)

Symptom: cascade mode ignores the standby pump. Workaround: restart the controller during the next maintenance window. Resolved in firmware 4.5.

## Issue CU4-KI-105 (affects firmware 4.5)

Symptom: OPC UA certificates expire after 365 days. Workaround: restart the controller during the next maintenance window. Resolved in firmware 4.6.

## Issue CU4-KI-106 (affects firmware 4.6)

Symptom: PID auto-tune overshoots on small loops. Workaround: restart the controller during the next maintenance window. Resolved in firmware 4.7.

## Issue CU4-KI-107 (affects firmware 4.7)

Symptom: alarm log truncates at 500 entries. Workaround: restart the controller during the next maintenance window. Resolved in firmware 4.8.

## Issue CU4-KI-108 (affects firmware 4.8)

Symptom: firmware update fails on 8 GB SD cards. Workaround: restart the controller during the next maintenance window. Resolved in firmware 4.9.
