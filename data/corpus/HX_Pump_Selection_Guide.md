# HX Pump Selection Guide

Guidance for sales engineers. Duty points below are typical operating points, not maximum ratings; see the technical specification for rated limits.

## HX-110

Typical duty point: 980 L/min at 68 m head. Best efficiency at the duty point is 62.7%.

Recommended spare-parts kit: SK-110-D. The HX-110 replaces the legacy model HX-105L.
